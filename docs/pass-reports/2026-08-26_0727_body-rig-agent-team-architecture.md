## 8. Combinatorial Impact

The new grammar permits profiles, poses, artwork, garments, region orientations, render relations, interactions, and panel placement to combine through stable semantic contracts. Garment pieces can cross joints without becoming skeleton parents; artwork can change without replacing mechanics; interactions can survive transfer; and new profiles can reuse pose/garment/anchor data.

Intentional restrictions prevent false combinations: limbs are region-atomic in v0.1, direct Front/Back edges are forbidden, unsupported art produces explicit fallback/warnings rather than silent substitution, and invalid orientation/render graphs cannot corrupt canonical state.
