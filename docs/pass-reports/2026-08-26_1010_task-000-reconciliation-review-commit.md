## 6. Major Architectural Conclusions

- Preserve semantic mechanical rig truth separately from final artwork and presentation resolution.
- Retain stable identities, hierarchy, state separation, Front ↔ 3/4 ↔ Back compatibility, semantic anchors, render relationships, and versioned transfer as the `2d-doll-rig-0.2` **DESIGNED candidate foundation**.
- Treat rigid articulated sprites as a necessary mechanical mechanism but not sufficient evidence of illustrative quality.
- Use compact semantic body-mass relationships and typed connection zones rather than hard-coded finished poses or unrestricted torso rotations.
- Treat localized correctives, masks, semantic depth, endpoint variants, and evidence-gated bounded deformation as replaceable presentation mechanisms, not skeleton truth.
- Preserve requested semantic state, derived/effective presentation, author overrides, and owner-approved values as distinguishable, reversible, provenance-bearing layers.
- Keep production artwork, exact control fields/limits, corrective counts, and general deformation **SPECULATIVE** pending bounded evidence and owner validation.
- Recommend, but do not implement, one 3/4 torso–pelvis illustrative-resolution spike before returning to broader regional-orientation work.

## 7. Combinatorial Impact

The design allows one semantic torso/pelvis relationship to combine with multiple profiles, view artwork, localized correctives, masks, semantic depth relations, garment signals, anchors, and reversible owner tuning. It preserves artwork replacement and later regional orientation while avoiding canonical complete-pose assets and scene-specific correction logic.

The principal intentional restriction is that mechanically legal states are not automatically presented as visually resolved. Unsupported presentation remains explicit, diagnosable, and non-destructive.
