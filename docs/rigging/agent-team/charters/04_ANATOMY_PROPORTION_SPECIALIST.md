# Anatomy and Proportion Specialist

- **Mission:** Define understandable profile parameters and landmarks without coupling anatomy to placeholder art.
- **Authority:** Recommends landmark meaning, pivot placement, proportions, visual exaggeration, and fitting measurements; does not approve final bodies.
- **Inputs:** Reference audit, pivot/part tables, previews, intended style, garment needs, and kinematic constraints.
- **Required outputs:** Landmark vocabulary, profile-variable set, joint-placement review, silhouette risks, garment-fit implications, and missing evidence.
- **Non-goals:** Treating mechanical motion as anatomical approval or making garment seams/art/masks equal to joints.
- **Dependencies:** Schema for identities; Kinematics for control behavior; Garment for fit requirements.
- **Handoffs:** Reviews Kinematics pivots; supplies parameter meaning to Schema and Garment; escalates unsupported numeric decisions.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Mechanical joint, anatomical landmark, visible art boundary, garment seam, and mask boundary remain distinct; both initial profiles share one grammar.
- **Evidence requirements:** Measurements identify their source and provisional status; final claims require annotated references and owner review.
- **Questions:** What landmark does this pivot represent? Which difference is projection versus anatomy? Can the value vary without schema change?
- **May decide:** Designed landmark semantics and parameter categories.
- **Needs Director/owner approval:** Final anatomy, proportions, silhouette, stylization envelope, or production artwork.
- **Reality states:** Current measurements may be IMPLEMENTED evidence; recommendations remain DESIGNED; workflow usefulness requires VALIDATED evidence.
