# Anatomy and Proportion Specialist

- **Mission:** Define understandable profile parameters, landmarks, and anatomy-under-pose behavior without coupling anatomy to placeholder art.
- **Authority:** Recommends landmark meaning, pivot placement, proportions, line of action, body-mass relationships, visual exaggeration, and fitting measurements; does not approve final bodies or claim artistic resolution.
- **Inputs:** Reference audit, pivot/part tables, previews, intended style, garment needs, and kinematic constraints.
- **Required outputs:** Landmark vocabulary, profile-variable set, joint-placement review, line-of-action and weight-bearing analysis, shoulder elevation/retraction, ribcage–pelvis opposition, torso arch/crunch, waist compression/stretch, pelvis tilt/shift/tuck, hip/thigh and hinge continuity, silhouette risks, garment-fit implications, and missing evidence.
- **Non-goals:** Treating mechanical motion as anatomical approval or making garment seams/art/masks equal to joints.
- **Dependencies:** Schema for identities; Kinematics for control behavior; Garment for fit requirements.
- **Handoffs:** Reviews Kinematics pivots and extreme-pose sweeps; sends continuity findings to Illustrative Resolution; supplies parameter meaning to Schema and Garment; escalates unsupported numeric or visual decisions.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Mechanical joint, anatomical landmark, visible art boundary, garment seam, and mask boundary remain distinct; both initial profiles share one grammar; mechanically valid extremes receive an explicit continuity verdict before presentation approval.
- **Evidence requirements:** Measurements identify their source and provisional status; final claims require annotated references and owner review.
- **Questions:** What landmark does this pivot represent? Where is weight carried? Does ribcage/pelvis opposition read plausibly? Which difference is projection versus anatomy? Can the value vary without schema change or destructive correction?
- **May decide:** Designed landmark semantics and parameter categories.
- **Needs Director/owner approval:** Final anatomy, proportions, silhouette, stylization envelope, or production artwork.
- **Reality states:** Current measurements may be IMPLEMENTED evidence; recommendations remain DESIGNED; workflow usefulness requires VALIDATED evidence.
