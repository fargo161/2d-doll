## Why this is highest leverage

Current source has matching semantic pivots and whole-body Front/3/4/Back assets, so mechanical regional attachment appears feasible. No evidence shows that mixed torso/pelvis/limb artwork seams, masks, depth, or correctives remain readable after conversion to local transforms. This uncertainty affects regional pose combinations, full-cycle shoulder/hip presentation, garments, layering, and later profile replacement.

## In scope

- Add pure data/model definitions for regional orientation state and the canonical compatibility/head matrices.
- Normalize inherited cropped asset/pivot metadata read-only through an adapter.
- Preserve current whole-body behavior as a set-all-regions compatibility mode.
- Prototype Front↔3/4 and 3/4↔Back at the waist plus one shoulder or hip branch using current provisional art.
- Show explicit legal, conditional, forbidden, missing-transition, and diagnostic-ghost states.
- Add atomic validation/error-code tests, local-transform equivalence regressions, and visual evidence.
- Record whether seam masks, offset corrections, depth relations, or corrective artwork are required.

## Out of scope

Male/female final profiles, final anatomy/art, full 360 visual support, garments, pose-transfer implementation, cloth physics, complete regional UI, and Placer remain excluded.

## Exit criterion

The pass answers one question with evidence: can the current common-pivot turnaround support declared regional bridge combinations through a reusable local-transform/transition contract, and what explicit corrective data is required? It must not promote provisional art or one successful seam as universal validation.
