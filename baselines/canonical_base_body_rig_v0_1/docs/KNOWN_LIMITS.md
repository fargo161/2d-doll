# Known Limits

1. This is a standardized technical base, not the final high-detail painted anatomy.
2. The Back anchor is constructed from rear-3/4 video references because the source set did not contain a clean straight-back neutral frame.
3. Large compound rotations can still expose stylization limits even when each individual joint remains inside its listed range.
4. Hands and feet are neutral base shapes. Gesture hands and footwear belong in later modular packs.
5. The 3/4 view is an authored orientation, not a mathematically rotated Front image.
6. No deformation mesh is used. Motion comes from separated rigid layers with hidden overlap.
7. Surface cleanup and additional corrective joint overlays can be added without changing IDs or hierarchy.
